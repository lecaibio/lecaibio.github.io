--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} Overrides minima 2.5.1's _layouts/home.html. Changes from the theme's version: - each post shows its front-matter `description` under the title (the same field also feeds jekyll-seo-tag's ) - each post shows its tags on the meta line, linking into /tags/ - word count and reading time sit under the description, not on the meta line, so they stay out of the way of the date and tags (see _includes/reading-time.html) - the theme's `site.show_excerpts` branch is dropped, since a written description reads better than an auto-clipped first paragraph - posts tagged `pin` are lifted into their own list above the chronological run Pinning is a plain tag, so nothing here needs a plugin or a second front-matter field. Jekyll builds site.tags itself, and site.tags.pin is already newest-first. The two lists need a heading each. Without one the front page would open on a list that is not in date order and looks like it lost its sort, and `pin` is hidden from the tag chips (see _includes/tag-list.html) so no per-post marker explains it. {%- endcomment -%}
